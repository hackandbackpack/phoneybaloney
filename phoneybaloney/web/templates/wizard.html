Welcome to PhoneyBaloney!

PhoneyBaloney is a vishing (voice phishing) simulator for security training. You'll have voice conversations with characters, practicing social engineering in a safe environment.

Let's get you set up. This wizard will walk you through configuring three components:

  • LLM — The brain that powers character conversations
  • TTS — Text-to-Speech for character voices
  • STT — Speech-to-Text for your microphone input

Everything can be changed later in Settings.

Step 2: Choose Your LLM

The LLM (Large Language Model) powers the intelligence behind each character's responses.

Step 3: Choose Your TTS

Text-to-Speech gives each character a voice you can hear through your speakers.

Step 4: Microphone & STT

Speech-to-Text listens to your microphone and converts your voice to text for the characters to understand.

You're All Set!

You can change any of these settings later from the Settings page.
